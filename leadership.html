LEADERSHIP

Pastor ​Frank Williams

Frank is a dynamic and charismatic man who leads by example. He is committed to being a servant leader and has a heart for people; evidenced through his heavy involvement in the welfare department of the church amongst his many other commitments.

He joined the RCCG in 1994 and was ordained as a Deacon in 1999, Assistant Pastor in 2005 and a Full Pastor in 2013. He has a direct and practical approach in his teaching style, and focuses heavily on hearing, adhering to and praying the word of God into one’s life as a recipe for successful Christian living. He is an ardent intercessor and hosts “Hour of Encounter”, a monthly interdenominational prayer assembly.
He is married to his lovely wife Remi and they are blessed with two children.

Pastor Remi Williams

Remi is a woman of action with exceptional organisational skills and a strong belief that all things should be done excellently. Ordained as a Deaconess in 2008, an Assistant Pastor in 2016 and a Full Pastor in 2023, she oversees a number of ministries in the Church and is a gifted administrator with a Master’s degree in Business Administration. She has a special affinity with mature singles and actively encourages them not to wait around but to keep attaining and achieving while waiting.

She is the convener of TTA (Time to Arise) Women’s Conference, an annual conference that impacts women from all works of life.
She stands firmly by her husband Frank and they are blessed with two children.
